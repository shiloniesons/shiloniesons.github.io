# Privacy Policy for WAvg

Effective date: April 2026

WAvg does not collect, store, or transmit any personal or sensitive user data.

All values and weights entered into the app are processed locally on the user’s device and are not sent to any external servers.

The app does not use analytics, advertising, tracking technologies, or third‑party SDKs that collect user data.

WAvg does not access contacts, location, camera, microphone, or any other personal device data.

If future updates introduce data collection or network features, this privacy policy will be updated accordingly.
